# fitness-subscription-analytics

The analysis for the company shows that the biggest amount of cancelations of subscriptions is after the 3rd month of starting a subscription. The January 2024 cohort had it's biggest drop from 122 subscribers in March to 78 in April. December 2024 cohort followed similarly with it's biggest drop from 261 in February 2025 to 167 subscribers in March 2025. As for the expected revenue over the next 12 months, we forsee a revenue of $235,088 in February of 2027 gradually growing around $320,616 by January of 2028. There is evidence of seasonality showing that normally there is a spike in income around December with a decline in January. Lastly, it takes just over 3 months for the 2024 cohorts to recover their customer acquisition cost and just over 5 months for the 2025 cohorts to recover.
